Problem: Students in Grade 6 Science could recite facts but couldn't explain why things happen.

Context: Grade 6, Science, 38 students

What was tried: Ran one lesson using a hands-on demo first, and only explained the science concept afterward, instead of lecturing first.

What happened: More students could explain the "why" in their own words afterward; a few quieter students still didn't join the group discussion part.

Tags: Grade 6, Science, Inquiry-based
