The Anubhav Loop

Anubhav (अनुभव) — Sanskrit and Hindi for lived, first-hand experience. The whole program is one repeating, four-phase mechanism.

1

Name

Teachers submit a live classroom problem. A 15-minute Global Practice Briefing, old and new practice, primes a Solution Design Sprint.

2

Try

Over 3–4 weeks, the plan runs in the teacher's actual classroom. No observer, no performance pressure — ordinary teaching, one deliberate change.

3

Capture

At least two lightweight evidence check-ins: a voice note, photo, or short video against three guided prompts. A buddy check-in at the midpoint.

4

Return

Teachers reconvene and report honestly, wins and failures both. Successes are minted into a credited Practice Card, added to the shared library.

Two Delivery Tiers, One Architecture

The identical four-phase loop runs at two resource levels, so the same design serves a low-connectivity rural school and a well-resourced urban one.

Foundation Tier

Low-connectivity schools

Paper Problem Ledger cards, WhatsApp voice notes for evidence capture, phone-call buddy check-ins, printed Practice Cards.

Enhanced Tier

Reliable connectivity & devices

Short video capture, AI-assisted reflection prompts, AI-drafted first-pass Practice Cards reviewed by a human before anything is published.
