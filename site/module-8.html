Cycle 8 of 8 · Weeks 43–48

Harvest

Consolidation, Showcase, and Next Year's Design

Strand: Collaborative Co-Researcher Foundation + Enhanced Tier ~5.5 contact hours

Learning Goals

  • Review the year's Practice Card library as a body of work, not scattered artifacts.
  • Publicly showcase the cycle each teacher is proudest of.
  • Nominate facilitators for next year from among this year's most active contributors.

Seed Problems

Illustrations only — a teacher's own live problem always takes priority in the Problem Ledger.

  • A strong Practice Card from Cycle 2 was never picked up or tried by anyone outside its original author's classroom.
  • The Problem Ledger has three recurring, unresolved problems that no single cycle fully solved this year.

Global Practice Briefing

The final cycle is deliberately not a new content area — it is where the compounding effect this whole design is built around either shows up or doesn't. Communities-of-practice research is clear that a domain survives past its founding only if the community actively curates and re-circulates its own knowledge; without a deliberate harvest step, even a strong module library quietly goes stale as cards are minted but never reread. Cycle 8 runs a showcase structure borrowed from Japan's public research lessons (koukai kenkyuu jugyou), where a school opens its best classroom work to a wider audience rather than keeping it internal — here, teachers present the cycle they are proudest of to the full cohort, including cards from other schools if the program has scaled to Phase 2. The cycle closes by explicitly naming which unresolved Problem Ledger items carry into next year, and by inviting veteran teachers who minted multiple cards this year into the facilitator pipeline described in Section 11 of the program design — succession made structural, not incidental.

What was tried
A 20-minute "card fair" at the Cycle 8 showcase where each teacher had to physically walk to, read, and sign one card that wasn't their own.
What happened
Every card in the year's library received at least one cross-classroom reader for the first time; three teachers committed on the spot to trying another teacher's card next year.
